## Divine Service

**Sunday Mornings at 9:00 AM**

All are welcome to join us as we worship Jesus Christ in the Divine Service. If you are visiting for the first time, feel free to come a few minutes early to get situated. Our parking lot - thanks be to God - is frequently full, feel free to park on Dale Ave if you cannot find a spot. If you plan on communing with us at the Lord's Table - or if you have questions regarding our position on the sacrament - feel free to make yourself known to our pastor before the service (he will be wearing the colorful vestments). As you enter our nave, our ushers will offer you a bulletin to help you navigate the service - it will direct you to the Lutheran Service Book and the Bible in your pew. Feel free to ask the ushers if you have questions. Don't forget to sign the guest book on the way out!

After the service, we will gather below the sanctuary for light refreshment and Bible study.

Please check our calendar for the most up to date service times for Feast Days and mid-week Evening Prayer and Vespers.
