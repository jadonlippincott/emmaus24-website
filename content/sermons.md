# Sermons

**And they said one to another, Did not our heart burn within us, while he talked with us by the way, and while he opened to us the scriptures?** — St. Luke 24:32

At Emmaus Evangelical Lutheran Church, the proclamation of God's Word stands at the heart of our life together. Each Sunday, our pastors preach from the appointed readings, opening the Holy Scriptures by the careful preaching of Law and Gospel - just as Christ did with his disciples on the Road to Emmaus.
