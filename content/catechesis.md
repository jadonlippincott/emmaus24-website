# Daily Catechesis on the Way

**These words that I command you today shall be on your heart. You shall teach them diligently to your children, and shall talk of them when you sit in your house, and when you walk by the way, and when you lie down, and when you rise.** — Deuteronomy 6:6-7

## A Weekly Catechetical Resource

_Daily Catechesis on the Way_ is a catechetical resource produced at Emmaus Evangelical Lutheran Church and published weekly since 2006. For nearly twenty years, this resource has served as a companion for the daily life of faith, guiding Christians through the riches of the Catechism, the Scriptures, the hymns of the Church, and the rhythm of the Church Year.

## What Is Catechesis?

The word _catechesis_ comes from the Greek and means "to sound down into the ears" -- to teach by repeating the faith until it is known by heart. Catechesis is not merely an intellectual exercise; it is the living, daily practice of hearing and learning God's Word so that it dwells richly in you and shapes your whole life in Christ.

From the earliest days of the Church, Christians have been catechized in the chief parts of the faith: the Ten Commandments, the Apostles' Creed, the Lord's Prayer, Holy Baptism, Confession and Absolution, and the Sacrament of the Altar. _Daily Catechesis on the Way_ continues this ancient practice by providing accessible, devotional material rooted in Luther's Small Catechism and the wider treasury of the Church's confession and prayer.

## Available Formats

_Daily Catechesis on the Way_ is available in two formats:

- **Online** -- Access the current and past weeks' catechetical materials right here on our website.
- **Print** -- A printed edition is also available for those who prefer to have a physical copy in hand for personal or family devotions.

## How to Use It

Each week's edition follows the rhythm of the Church Year and the appointed lectionary, providing readings, catechetical texts, hymn stanzas, and prayers for each day of the week. It is designed to be used by individuals, families, and study groups alike. Whether at the breakfast table, during a quiet moment in the evening, or as part of your daily prayer routine, _Daily Catechesis on the Way_ is intended to keep the Word of God before you each and every day.

We pray that this resource is a blessing to you and your household as you walk together on the way of faith.
